Power Grid Operator Exam Prep · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Privacy Policy

Power Grid Operator Exam Prep (shown as “Grid Prep”, formerly System Operator Prep) does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, and no server. The only network traffic is Apple's own App Store handling the optional Full Access purchase.

What the app collects

Nothing. Power Grid Operator Exam Prep contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. It does not ask for your name, email address, employer, or any other personal detail, because it has nowhere to send them.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your study data so your progress survives between sessions. This includes:

  • which questions you have answered and whether you answered them correctly,
  • the spaced-review schedule built from those answers,
  • your mock paper scores and timings, and which credential you have selected.

All of it is written to a single file inside the app's own storage on your device — on a Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. It never leaves the device, it is not backed up to any server belonging to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with "Reset all progress" in the Library tab, and deleting the app deletes it too.

Network access

The app itself makes no network requests. Every question, reference card and explanation is bundled inside the app, the one-line diagrams, contingency analysis reports, AGC control boards and calculation drills are generated on the device, and everything works entirely offline. There is no server component.

The optional Full Access purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit): Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your device whether the purchase exists. The developer receives no personal or payment information, and the app keeps only a count of free questions and mock papers used, on your device.

Children

Power Grid Operator Exam Prep is a study aid for grid control-room operators and is rated 4+. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
