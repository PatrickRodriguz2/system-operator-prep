NERC System Operator exam: free practice questions

Sample questions from Power Grid Operator Exam Prep, grouped by the six domains of the NERC System Operator Certification content outline (RC, BT, TO and BI). Every answer is explained and cites its source.

  • Resource and Demand Balancing (5 questions): Keeping generation, load and interchange in balance: schedules, reserves, AGC, ACE and frequency.
  • Transmission (5 questions): The wires side: protection, voltage and reactive control, switching, equipment and operating limits.
  • Emergency Preparedness (3 questions): Getting ahead of the next shift — day-ahead and same-day studies, severe weather, GMD and capacity shortfalls.
  • Emergency Response (5 questions): Acting when the system is already in trouble: disturbances, capacity emergencies, restoration, and losing your own tools.
  • Contingency Analysis and Reliability (5 questions): Studying the next contingency before it happens, and operating inside SOLs and IROLs.
  • Communications and Data (4 questions): Three-part communication, reporting obligations, telemetry and the quality of the data the decisions rest on.
