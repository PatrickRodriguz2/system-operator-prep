Communications and Data: practice questions

Three-part communication, reporting obligations, telemetry and the quality of the data the decisions rest on. Domain 6 of the NERC System Operator Certification content outline. Each answer is explained and cites its source.

Question 1 · Reporting Requirements

A Transmission Operator recognizes at 09:00 on a Friday that an event has met an EOP-004-4 Attachment 1 threshold. The entity's business days are Monday through Friday. The report is due:

  1. by 4 p.m. on Monday
  2. by 9 a.m. on Saturday
  3. by 4 p.m. on Friday
  4. by 9 a.m. on Monday
Show the answer

Answer: A. R2 requires the later of 24 hours after recognition or the end of the next business day. Twenty-four hours lands at 09:00 Saturday, while the next business day ends Monday at 4 p.m. local time, which the standard treats as the end of the business day, so Monday 4 p.m. controls.

Reference: EOP-004-4 R2

Question 2 · Communication Methods

A Transmission Operator is assembling its COM-001-3 evidence. With which entity must it have Interpersonal Communication capability while NOT being required to designate an Alternative Interpersonal Communication capability?

  1. A Generator Operator inside its Transmission Operator Area
  2. A Balancing Authority inside its Transmission Operator Area
  3. An adjacent Transmission Operator that is synchronously connected
  4. The Reliability Coordinator for its area
Show the answer

Answer: A. Requirement R3 puts Generator Operators and Distribution Providers inside the Transmission Operator Area on the primary capability list, but Requirement R4 narrows the alternative capability to the Reliability Coordinator, the Balancing Authorities in the area, and adjacent Transmission Operators connected synchronously or asynchronously. The other three options appear on both lists, so each already has a designated alternative.

Reference: COM-001-3 R3 and R4

Question 3 · Data Validity and Verification

A Generator Operator disagrees with the reporting deadline its Reliability Coordinator has written into the data specification it received. How is that disagreement meant to be settled?

  1. By using the mutually agreeable process the specification identifies
  2. By referring the matter to the Regional Entity for a binding ruling
  3. By withholding the data until the Reliability Coordinator revises the deadline
  4. By applying whichever deadline the Generator Operator's own systems support
Show the answer

Answer: A. IRO-010-5 R1 Part 1.4 requires the specification itself to identify a mutually agreeable process for resolving conflicts, so that process is the route. Withholding data is not an option, because R3 obliges the receiving entity to satisfy the obligations of the documented specification.

Reference: IRO-010-5 R1 Part 1.4; IRO-010-5 R3

Question 4 · Telemetry and Communications Equipment

A metering current transformer has a ratio of 1200:5. The secondary ammeter at the relay panel reads 4.2 A. What primary current is flowing in the circuit?

  1. 1,008 A
  2. 1,200 A
  3. 1,050 A
  4. 5,040 A
Show the answer

Answer: A. The turns ratio is 1200 / 5 = 240, so the primary current is 4.2 x 240 = 1,008 A. 5,040 A comes from multiplying the 4.2 A reading by the full 1,200 A primary rating instead of by the ratio, 1,050 A from rounding the ratio to 250, and 1,200 A is the nameplate rating, which the circuit carries only at a 5 A secondary reading.

Reference: Power system fundamentals
